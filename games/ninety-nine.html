Answers: Rio Run (Snake)

Their cabinet is Snake, with a figure resembling the President running the Rio Grande and gathering up border crossers. The trail behind you is a tally of people collected. Growth is capture.

Ours keeps the trail and inverts the errand. You are a shepherd. The thing behind you is a flock, and you are responsible for every one of them until they are through the gate.

THE ARGUMENT

The oldest political image in the West is a shepherd, and it is not a flattering one for rulers. The shepherd is judged by whether the sheep get home — not by how many he rounded up, and not by how fast.

So this cabinet keeps score two ways. You get a little for finding someone. You get a great deal for leading them somewhere safe. And you can lose them: there is a wolf, and if you trample your own flock in your hurry, the run is over. That is the whole difference between a patrol and a pastorate.

“What man of you, having a hundred sheep, if he has lost one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go after the one which is lost, until he finds it?” Luke 15:4
