Answers: Trump Savings Tycoon

Their cabinet has you catching money out of the air to fill a government account opened in your child's name — an account branded with the name of the man who signed it into being. The money arrives from above. Nobody asks where it came from or what it is for.

Ours keeps the basket and the falling coins, and adds the two questions their version leaves out: where did this come from, and what is it for?

THE ARGUMENT

The parable of the talents is not a parable about accumulation. The servant who is condemned is the one who kept his master's money perfectly safe and did nothing with it. Stewardship means the money is for something, and you will be asked.

So this cabinet makes you split every wage three ways while you are still working. Giving is not a leak in the bucket — enough of it makes a neighbour, and neighbours send work back to you, which is exactly how a civil society actually functions and exactly what a transfer program cannot manufacture.

And then there are the subsidies. They are real money and they are easy to catch. Each one adds a string, and every string narrows your basket for the rest of the run and skims a little off the top forever. That is not a joke about welfare. It is the oldest observation in classical liberalism: nothing the state hands you arrives without a hand still attached to it.
