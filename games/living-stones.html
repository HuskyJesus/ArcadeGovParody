Answers: Build the Wall (Tetris)

Their cabinet turns Tetris into a border wall and calls the people on the far side of it the coming horde — a rising quantity to be held back, and never a face.

Ours keeps the skill exactly: fit awkward shapes together under time pressure. It only changes what you are building and who is on the other side of it.

THE ARGUMENT

There is nothing wrong with a nation having a border, and nothing wrong with a law that says who may cross it. Every serious tradition, ours included, takes that for granted.

What is wrong is the picture. Teach a child that the people beyond the wall are an undifferentiated mass, and you have taught them the one thing Scripture spends four books arguing against: that a person can be a statistic. Every stone in this cabinet is a stone in someone's house — because that is what stones are for, and because a nation that only knows how to build barriers eventually forgets how to build anything else.
