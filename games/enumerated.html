Answers: Flappy Bill (Flappy Bird)

Their cabinet flies a bald eagle carrying a bill down the National Mall. There is no committee, no chamber, no court, no objection — the bill simply travels, and the only obstacles are scenery.

Ours keeps the eagle, the bill, and the one-button flight. It restores the obstacles the founders put in the way on purpose, and names them.

THE ARGUMENT

Every gate in this cabinet is a veto point somebody deliberately built: committee, floor debate, two chambers that have to agree, a president who can refuse, courts that can strike, and an electorate that gets the last word. In the civics of their arcade these are absent. In ours they are the entire game.

The weight mechanic makes the second half of the argument. A bill that carries a subsidy for one firm, a museum named for its sponsor, an exemption for its own authors — that bill is heavier, and it deserves to be. A short bill that does one enumerated thing flies. This is not cynicism about legislating. It is the reason the process was made hard in the first place.
