In September 2026 the White House published an arcade. Five browser games under a federal seal: a Tetris variant about building a border wall against what the game calls the coming horde, a Snake variant about rounding up border crossers along the Rio Grande, a sorting game about approving school lunches against an administration slogan, a Flappy Bird variant in which an eagle carries a bill triumphantly down the National Mall, and a coin-catcher about filling a government savings account branded with the name of the man who created it. A sixth cabinet sits empty, promising that a new adventure awaits.

It would be easy to treat this as beneath comment. We think that is exactly the mistake. Nobody remembers a press release. Everybody remembers what they had to do with their hands ten thousand times to get a high score.

WHY A GAME IS NOT A POSTER

A poster asks you to agree with a claim. A game asks you to enact one, over and over, and rewards you for getting better at it. That is a fundamentally different kind of persuasion, and a far more durable one. The mechanic is the message: what the game makes you good at is what the game believes.

So look at what these five make you good at. Stacking a barrier faster than a faceless mass can arrive. Growing longer by collecting human beings. Judging what other people may eat against a standard handed down from above. Flying legislation past every obstacle without friction — because in this telling, obstacles to the will of the executive are merely scenery. And catching money that falls from the sky with a politician's name printed on it.

Each of those is a small, repeated lesson about who counts, who decides, and where good things come from. Played once, they're trivial. That is the point of a game. It is never played once.

SO WE BUILT THE ANSWER, MECHANIC FOR MECHANIC

We did not build parodies. A parody borrows the target's frame and mocks it, and you end up arguing on their terms. We built counters: the same verb, the same skill, the same muscle memory, aimed at a different end. If a mechanic can teach you that people are a rising quantity to be held back, the identical mechanic can teach you that a stone is for a roof. The skill is neutral. The object of the skill never is.

  • Living Stones keeps Tetris exactly and changes what you're building. A cleared row is not a repelled wave; it's a household with a name, taken in.
  • The Ninety-Nine keeps Snake's growing trail and makes it a flock you are answerable for — and adds a gate, standing open, that you have to lead them through.
  • Honest Scales keeps the conveyor and the two-key sort, and makes you the merchant instead of the censor. You may refuse a lie. You may not refuse an honest man because you feel like it.
  • Enumerated keeps the eagle and the one-button flight and restores every check the founders put in the way on purpose. Pork makes the bill heavy. Struck clauses make it fly.
  • Ten Talents keeps the basket and the falling coins and adds the two questions the original omits: where did this come from, and what is it for?

WHAT WE ACTUALLY BELIEVE

Two traditions built the free world, and they are not in conflict. One says every person is made in the image of God, which is why no person may be reduced to a quantity. The other says power is dangerous in every hand including good ones, which is why it must be divided, checked, enumerated, and made to answer. Between them they produce a short list of convictions, and every cabinet on this floor is built on one of them.

1Persons are not populations.

You may argue about immigration levels, enforcement, and law — those are legitimate political questions with serious answers on more than one side. What you may not do is teach a child that the people on the other side of a line are an undifferentiated mass. Every named family in Living Stones and every named sheep in The Ninety-Nine exists to make one point: a policy can be about aggregates, but a soul never is.

“You shall not wrong a stranger or oppress him, for you were strangers in the land of Egypt.” Exodus 22:21

2Honest weights, or nothing.

The market case and the biblical case land in the same place. Exchange between free people is how strangers cooperate without a commissar — but the whole thing rests on the label being true. Fraud isn't commerce; it's the thing commerce survives despite. Note which one the older law regulates: not what you may sell, but the scales you sell it with.

3Friction is a feature.

Enumerated exists because a government arcade showed legislation sailing through unopposed and called that a win. Committees, chambers, courts, and elections are not obstacles to democracy. They are democracy's load-bearing walls, and they were placed there by men who assumed — correctly — that their own side would one day want them gone.

“If men were angels, no government would be necessary. If angels were to govern men, neither external nor internal controls on government would be necessary.” James Madison, Federalist 51

4Charity is voluntary, or it isn't charity.

Ten Talents pays you for giving — not because giving is a clever investment strategy, but because it makes neighbours, and neighbours are the actual substance of a free society. A transfer administered from a capital can move money. It cannot manufacture the thing that happens between two people when one of them chose.

5Nothing the state hands you comes without a hand attached.

The subsidy crates in Ten Talents are real money and easy to catch, and each one adds a visible string that shrinks your basket for the rest of the run. Not a joke about welfare — an observation about dependence, and about why an account with a politician's name on it is a strange thing to teach a child to want.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

This is not an attack on anyone's character, a claim that games in government are inherently sinister, or an argument that only one party is capable of this. Administrations of every stripe have reached for the fun, frictionless format when they wanted a lesson to go down easy. The objection is to the specific lessons, and it would be the same objection if the seal were a different colour.

It is also not a demand that anything be taken down. The answer to a game is another game. That's the whole classical liberal proposition, and it's why this site exists rather than a petition.
